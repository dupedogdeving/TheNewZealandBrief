Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 4 September 2026

We operate this news website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our website.

We do not run independent website analytics. However, our website displays advertisements through Google AdSense, which uses cookies and data collection to serve ads.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

While we do not actively collect personal information for analytics, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. The data processed by these cookies may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • The pages of our site that you visit
  • The time and date of your visit

2. Google AdSense and Cookies

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

Personalised Advertising: By default, Google may use cookies to personalise the ads you see.

Opting Out: You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Contact Forms and User Communications

If you contact us directly via email or through a contact form on our website, we may receive additional information about you, such as your name, email address, and the contents of the message you send us. We only use this information to respond directly to your inquiry. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. Our website uses HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
