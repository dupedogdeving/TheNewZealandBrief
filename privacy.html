Privacy Statement

Last Updated: 4 September 2026

```

The New Zealand Brief ("we", "us", or "our") operates this website as an online news and information publication. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be collected, used and stored when you visit our website.

This Privacy Statement explains the types of information that may be processed through the website, why it may be processed, and the services that may be involved.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, information that may be processed can include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, such as your name, email address and message.
  • Technical information associated with your visit, such as IP address, browser type, device information and pages requested.
  • Information stored in or associated with browser cookies and similar technologies.
  • An anonymous identifier stored in a browser cookie that may be used to estimate the number of currently active visitors.

2. Live Visitor Count

The website may use a first-party cookie containing a randomly generated anonymous visitor identifier. This identifier is used for website functionality that allows the site owner to estimate the number of visitors who are currently active on the website.

The live visitor count is intended to provide an approximate number of active visitors. It is not intended to identify individual visitors or determine their identity.

The website may send the anonymous identifier and a recent activity timestamp to our Firebase/Google Cloud database service so that the owner-only live visitor count can operate across visitors and devices.

The live visitor count is available only to authorised website administrators and is not displayed publicly as an individual visitor list.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored by a website in your browser. We may use cookies for essential website functionality, preferences and the anonymous live visitor-count feature described above.

You can normally control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Our website may also contain third-party technologies that use their own cookies or similar technologies.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

We may display advertising through Google AdSense or other advertising services. These services may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to deliver, measure or improve advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google may process information associated with advertising activity in accordance with its own privacy policies and settings. We do not control the information collected directly by Google or other advertising providers.

More information about how Google uses data when you use its partners' websites can be found through Google's explanation of how it uses data .

You can manage Google's advertising personalisation settings through Google Ads Settings .

You can also learn more about opting out of certain interest-based advertising through YourAdChoices .

5. Firebase and Website Services

The website uses Firebase and related Google Cloud services to provide certain website functionality, including authentication, database storage and website administration features.

Information processed by these services may be stored or processed on infrastructure operated by Google. The information processed depends on how the website is used and which features are active.

We use these services to operate and maintain the website and do not intend to use the live visitor identifier to determine the real-world identity of ordinary website visitors.

6. Contacting Us

If you contact us by email or through a contact feature on the website, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

We use this information primarily to respond to your enquiry, communicate with you, or deal with the matter you contacted us about.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

7. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Providing website features and services.
  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing abuse.
  • Understanding basic website operation and visitor activity where applicable.
  • Displaying and measuring advertising where advertising services are enabled.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may nevertheless be processed by service providers that help us operate the website, including hosting, database, authentication, advertising and email service providers.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by law, including where necessary to protect the website, its users or our legal rights.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for website administration, security, legal obligations, or legitimate operational requirements.

Anonymous live-visitor presence information is intended to represent recent activity rather than a permanent record of individual visits.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information held by or on behalf of the website. The website may use HTTPS encryption and third-party security infrastructure.

However, no website, online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable New Zealand privacy law, you may have rights to request access to personal information we hold about you and to request correction of information that is inaccurate.

If you would like to make a privacy request or ask a question about information we hold, please contact us using the details below.

12. Children's Privacy

The website is intended to provide news and information to the general public. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us unnecessarily, please contact us so that we can review the information and take appropriate action where required.

13. Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence tools may be used to assist with the development, coding, design, maintenance or other technical work involved in creating and operating this website.

The use of AI development tools does not mean that those tools automatically receive or have access to information about every visitor to this website. Any information processed by third-party services is described elsewhere in this statement according to the services actually used by the website.

Website operators remain responsible for reviewing and managing the website and its content.

14. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of those third-party websites.

You should review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

15. Changes to This Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement when our website, services or privacy practices change. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when the statement is updated.
