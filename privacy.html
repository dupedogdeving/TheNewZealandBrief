Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

The New Zealand Brief is a small, independent news project. This policy explains what information the site collects when you read it, why, and what choices you have. We keep the amount of data we collect to the minimum needed to run the site well.

What we collect

Reading the site as a visitor involves very little data collection:

  • Cookie consent choice — whether you accepted or declined cookies, so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • Last category browsed — only stored if you accept cookies, so the site can reopen on the section you were reading.
  • Standard hosting logs — like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as IP address and browser type, used only for security and reliability.

None of this information is used for advertising, cross-site tracking, or analytics, and none of it is sold or shared with third parties.

Owner / editor accounts

The site has a small number of editor accounts used to publish stories. Sign-in for these accounts is handled by Firebase Authentication. If you are not a site editor, this does not apply to you — there is no public sign-up, and reading the site never requires an account.

Cookies

We use a small, first-party cookie to remember your cookie preference, and — only if you accept — a second cookie to remember the story category you were browsing. Neither cookie is used for advertising or analytics, and neither is shared with anyone else. You can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

Advertising

Some pages on this site reserve space for advertising. Where ads are shown, they may be served by a third-party advertising network, which can set its own cookies subject to its own privacy policy. We do not control the data practices of third-party ad networks and recommend reviewing their policies directly if you have concerns.

Your choices

You can:

  • Decline non-essential cookies at any time from the cookie banner or cookie settings.
  • Clear your browser's cookies to remove any stored preference.
  • Browse the site fully without an account — reading never requires sign-in.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the site editor.

The New Zealand Brief is a personal, independent news project based in Aotearoa New Zealand.
